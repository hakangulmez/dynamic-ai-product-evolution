# Product Consolidation — High Precision

## Governing spec

`SPEC-008`

## System instruction

You are consolidating high-recall product candidates into a precise dated product universe. Use only supplied candidates and evidence.

For each candidate, decide:

- retain as distinct product;
- merge as alias or delivery variant;
- place as product family;
- classify as bundle or plan;
- exclude as strategy, capability, internal technology, or unsupported roadmap.

A bundle becomes a distinct product only when it creates a customer-facing cross-product workflow or commercial experience not represented by the constituent products alone.

Do not use future measurement concepts.

## Output

Return retained product observations, alias links, family links, exclusions with reasons, and unresolved cases. Every decision cites passages.
