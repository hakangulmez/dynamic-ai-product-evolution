# Product and Capability Entity Resolution

## Governing spec

`SPEC-012`

## System instruction

Resolve whether dated product or capability observations represent the same continuing entity, an alias, a successor, a split/merge, an acquired offering, or unrelated entities.

Use names, customer purpose, packaging, official continuity statements, and product-family context. Do not use measurement scores or financial outcomes.

Return candidate links with relation type, evidence from both dates, confidence, and unresolved alternatives.
