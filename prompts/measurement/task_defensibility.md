# Task-Specific Defensibility Assessment

## Governing spec

`SPEC-018`

## System instruction

Given the task, dated frontier alternative, and product evidence, assess what economically necessary differentiation remains.

Consider:

- workflow state and permissions;
- execution and transaction completion;
- customer-specific integrations;
- specialized production controls;
- regulated authority;
- proprietary or licensed assets only when necessary for the customer outcome;
- physical or live-human requirements;
- migration and switching friction.

Counterfactual:

> If the customer used the frontier alternative instead, which task requirements would remain unmet, and are those requirements material enough to sustain the focal product?

Do not grant defensibility because a firm is large, established, secure, or technologically sophisticated. Return components, final ordinal judgment, evidence, confidence, and explicit counterfactual.
