# Glossary

**Capability** — A concrete product function that enables one or more customer tasks.

**Customer-facing task** — An economically meaningful job the customer uses the product to accomplish, written as a verb, object, and intended outcome.

**Customer need** — The underlying objective that may be satisfied by the focal product or an alternative.

**Product family** — A stable commercial grouping containing related products.

**Product** — An identifiable offering that customers can buy, license, subscribe to, deploy, or use.

**Task observation** — A dated record that a product capability supported a customer task with specific evidence and availability status.

**Task transition** — A longitudinal relation between task observations, such as unchanged, expanded, AI-assisted, agentified, replaced, merged, split, or discontinued.

**Frontier Task Replicability** — The extent to which the frontier general-purpose system available at the observation date can satisfy the underlying customer task without the focal product.

**AI Transformation Depth** — The depth of AI integration from no concrete use through assistance, generation, workflow integration, action execution, and orchestration.

**Deployment Scale** — The breadth and commercial reality of a transformation across availability, workflows, products, customers, and monetization.

**Task-Specific Defensibility** — Differentiation that remains economically necessary despite the frontier alternative.

**Observation cutoff** — The latest date of information allowed to support an observation.

**Official source** — A source published or filed by the focal company or regulator, under the hierarchy in `SOURCE_POLICY.md`.

**Unknown** — Evidence is insufficient. Unknown is not equivalent to zero.
