# Measurement Design — Provisional

## Status

This document defines candidate construct families and required evidence. No composite score is frozen.

## 1. Frontier Task Replicability

Question:

> At the observation date, could the frontier general-purpose system satisfy the task's underlying customer need without the focal product at comparable practical quality?

Candidate ordinal scale:

- 0 — not meaningfully capable
- 1 — assistive only
- 2 — partial substitute
- 3 — near substitute
- 4 — direct substitute with low friction

Assessment dimensions:

- modality and output quality;
- reasoning and domain knowledge;
- tool use and external data;
- end-to-end completeness;
- latency and cost;
- need for firm-specific assets;
- switching friction.

## 2. AI Transformation Depth

Candidate ladder:

- 0 — no concrete integration or marketing-only language
- 1 — peripheral assistance, search, summary, drafting
- 2 — direct generation, classification, recommendation
- 3 — native workflow integration with product context or state
- 4 — multi-step action execution through tools or APIs
- 5 — goal-directed orchestration, monitoring, and exception handling

Depth is task-specific. A company-level statement never determines the score.

## 3. Deployment Scale

Keep separate components initially:

- availability status;
- feature breadth;
- workflow breadth;
- product breadth;
- customer-deployment evidence;
- commercialization and pricing;
- usage or revenue evidence.

Do not collapse these before pilot reliability is known.

## 4. Task-Specific Defensibility

Question:

> After considering the frontier alternative, what task-specific advantages remain economically necessary and difficult to replace?

Candidate evidence:

- workflow state and permissions;
- customer-specific integrations;
- execution rights and transaction completion;
- proprietary or licensed data that materially improves the customer outcome;
- specialized production controls;
- regulated authority;
- human network or physical presence;
- switching and migration costs.

The existence of proprietary data is insufficient. The evaluator must explain why the customer still needs it after the frontier alternative.

## 5. Task Economic Importance

Suggested labels:

- core
- major supporting
- peripheral

Revenue weights may be added where disclosed, but missing revenue data must not be imputed from text prominence.

## Derived measures

Any derived index must:

- state the theory;
- preserve component outputs;
- pass sensitivity analysis;
- avoid treating transformation as benefit by definition;
- be validated on out-of-sample anchors.
