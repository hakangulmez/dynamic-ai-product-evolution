# Aggregation Policy — Provisional

## Principle

Task-level observations are primary. Aggregation is a reporting decision, not a repair mechanism.

## Required released levels

- task-year
- capability-year
- product-year
- product-family-year
- firm-year

## Role weighting

No role weights are frozen. Candidate approaches include:

- equal task weights within product;
- ordinal role weights;
- disclosed revenue weights;
- multiple reported aggregations.

## Hierarchical balance

Aggregation should prevent arbitrary task splitting from dominating firm-level values. The pilot must compare task-weighted, product-balanced, and product-family-balanced results.

## Missingness

Unknown measurement values remain missing. Do not treat unknown as zero. Report coverage and uncertainty with aggregates.

## Dynamic aggregation

Firm-year change may reflect:

- within-task score change;
- task entry;
- task exit;
- product mix change;
- acquisition or divestiture.

Decomposition tables should separate these channels.
