# Adversarial Universe Boundary Cases

These are design examples for the future eval harness. Each must be converted into a structured eval case with source IDs and expected fields before production use.

## 1. Internal AI use

> We deploy artificial intelligence and proprietary software throughout our manufacturing operations.

Expected: no customer-facing functional software product from this passage alone.

## 2. Mobile application

> Customers use our mobile application to purchase products and track delivery.

Expected: digital interface does not establish core software; likely e-commerce/retail or enabling layer.

## 3. Physical marketplace

> The platform connects riders with independent drivers and facilitates payment.

Expected: `MARKETPLACE_COORDINATION`, software `ENABLING`, physical supply network required, Tier C candidate.

## 4. Content catalogue

> Subscribers stream our licensed and produced films and series across connected devices.

Expected: `CONTENT_CATALOG`; licensed content necessary; Tier C candidate unless a separate material functional product is established.

## 5. Functional analytics with licensed data

> Customers query, combine, monitor, and analyze market data through our workstation to support investment decisions.

Expected: `DATA_ANALYTICS_PRODUCT`, likely `CO_ESSENTIAL`; licensed/proprietary data is an input rather than passive entertainment content.

## 6. Human-managed service

> Consultants design, implement, and operate client technology systems using our internal accelerators.

Expected: `HUMAN_MANAGED_SERVICE`; internal accelerators do not establish a software product without evidence of a separately commercial offering.

## 7. Transaction infrastructure

> Merchants use our platform to authorize, route, settle, and reconcile payments.

Expected: `TRANSACTION_INFRASTRUCTURE`; regulated rails, network, and execution permissions considered; Tier B candidate.

## 8. Game company

> Players purchase and participate in interactive entertainment worlds and online multiplayer experiences.

Expected: `INTERACTIVE_ENTERTAINMENT`; retain in Tier C boundary stratum.

## 9. Hardware/software system

> The device and embedded software continuously monitor patients and adjust treatment delivery.

Expected: `HARDWARE_SOFTWARE_SYSTEM`, software `CO_ESSENTIAL`, hardware dependency; Tier B or C pending materiality and research scope.

## 10. Adaptive digital service

> Learners submit questions and receive individualized step-by-step explanations and practice materials.

Expected: `ADAPTIVE_DIGITAL_SERVICE`, software likely `CORE` or `CO_ESSENTIAL`; Tier A candidate when materiality is supported.

## 11. Generic platform language

> Our innovative cloud platform empowers customers to accelerate digital transformation.

Expected: insufficient concrete evidence; remain uncertain or ineligible from this passage alone.

## 12. Mixed separable firm

> The company reports a cloud-software segment and a substantially larger physical retail and logistics segment separately.

Expected: `MIXED_SEPARABLE`; product analysis may include the software segment, but total-firm outcome use requires a segment mapping.
