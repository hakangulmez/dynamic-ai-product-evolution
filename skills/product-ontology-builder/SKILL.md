# Product Ontology Builder Skill

## Purpose

Construct product families, products, and aliases from validated sources.

## Required reading

- `SPEC-008`
- `PRODUCT_CAPABILITY_TASK_ONTOLOGY.md`

## Procedure

1. Run high-recall discovery.
2. Consolidate packaging and aliases.
3. Assign availability and acquisition status.
4. Validate evidence.

## Output discipline

- Use the governing schema.
- Cite source and passage IDs.
- Preserve unknowns and ambiguity.
- Write concise audit rationales, not hidden chain-of-thought.
- Produce a run manifest.

## Stop conditions

- Product boundary remains unresolved after consolidation.
